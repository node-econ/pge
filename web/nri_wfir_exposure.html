Notes

  1. FEMA National Risk Index census tracts (PGE clip). Table 1 groups tracts by WFIR_RISKR (wildfire risk category), highest risk first. WFIR_EXPB is building exposure — tract values are summed; amounts are in millions of USD in the source layer (table shows $ with comma grouping). Source attributes: NRI_Census_Tracts_PGE.dbf. Map: tract boundaries from NRI_Census_Tracts_PGE.geojson (EPSG:4326, simplified) over OpenStreetMap. Not a FEMA publication; confirm definitions in the NRI technical documentation.
  2. Table 2 aggregates WFIR_EALT (expected annual loss from wildfire) across tracts in each county. Counties use the tract attribute STCOFIPS (state + county FIPS); the County column uses COUNTY and STATEABBRV from the same NRI tract record when present. This matches the tract → county geography in the published GeoJSON used on the map.
